Throwaway prototype, not the product

Agent cards prototype

The question: can our AI model turn a plain-English task into a good agent card, and can the Room lead pick the right saved agents for a new task?

An agent card is data, not code: a name, a purpose, a plan, the tools it may use (only from a fixed list), and how many people must approve each write. The platform picks who. The "Checks" under each card are what the platform would refuse or flag before anyone could save it.
