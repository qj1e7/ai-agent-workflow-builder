# Task cards

Hand these over one at a time. The card is what the person reads. The questions under each card are for the facilitator to ask afterwards.

After every task, ask the same two rating questions:
- **How easy or hard was that?** 1 is very hard and 7 is very easy.
- **How sure are you that it will do what you wanted?** 1 is not sure at all and 7 is completely sure.

---

## 1. First visit (scenario A)

> **Card.** It's your first day using this tool. Every Friday, your team writes a summary of the Jira tickets it closed that week. Get the tool to do this for you every Friday afternoon.
>
> If it asks you something you don't know, use project MOCK and 4 pm.

Backup wording, if the box doesn't understand: "every Friday, sum up the tickets we closed this week".

Afterwards ask:
- What will happen every Friday?
- Will anything change in Jira? Does anyone have to agree first?

---

## 2. Watch software (scenario G)

> **Card.** Your team runs Kubernetes. Make sure you hear about new versions of it.

Backup wording: "Monitor Kubernetes releases".

Afterwards ask:
- Where would you look later to see if there's a new version?
- What does the tool know about the version your team runs?

---

## 3. The tool guessed wrong (scenario E)

> **Card.** Type exactly this: *Every Friday at 17:00, sum up the tickets we closed this week in project MOCK*
>
> Then your manager says it should be for project OPS, and on Mondays instead. Change it without starting again.

Afterwards ask:
- How did you know your changes were taken in?

---

## 4. Something it can't do (scenario D)

> **Card.** You'd like a list of the open bugs emailed to you every Monday. Try to set that up.

Backup wording: "email me a list of open bugs every Monday".

Afterwards ask:
- What happened? Why couldn't it do that?
- What would you do now?

---

## 5. What needs me today (scenario B)

> **Card.** It's Monday morning and you've just opened the tool. Find out whether anything needs your attention, and deal with the most urgent thing.

Afterwards ask:
- What happened this morning? Was anything changed in Jira?

---

## 6. A job stopped (scenario F)

> **Card.** Two of your team's regular jobs have stopped. For each one, find out why. Fix it yourself if you can. If you can't, pass it on to the people who look after the tool.

Afterwards ask:
- Which one could you fix yourself, and how did you know?
- What happens next with the one you passed on?

---

## 7. Change, pause and remove (scenario H)

> **Card.** Three changes to make:
> 1. The Morning new-ticket check should run at 9:00 instead of 8:00.
> 2. Then stop it running for now.
> 3. Your team doesn't need the Weekly closed-ticket summary any more. Remove it.

Afterwards ask:
- How would you start the morning check again later?
- Could you get the weekly summary back?

---

## 8. Approve a change (scenario C)

The facilitator switches to Bob first. Tell the person: "You are now Bob. Bob checks and approves changes. He doesn't set things up."

> **Card.** You've been asked to check some changes to Jira. Decide whether to allow them.

Afterwards ask:
- What exactly will change if you say yes?
- Who asked for this, and why?
- If you say no, what happens?
