What’s new in version 3

  • Where you came from decides. From the rail, Workflows and the Watchlist open as full pages, and the chat shows only on Chat. From a card in the chat, the first click opens beside it, and anything you open from there goes full page, with Back to the chat. A flow shows once: while it is open beside the chat, the card doesn’t repeat it. Play “Browse”, then “Morning check”.
  • The side panel is a summary: what it is, its state, and the next step. More details opens the full page, which adds what helps you understand it, such as a run’s step-by-step log or a release’s notes. An approval always shows everything that will be written.
  • Every part of a request gets a tick or a cross, with a reason. Nothing is dropped, and a missing time is asked, never guessed.
  • “Make the React check run on Mondays only” changes the workflow you have. You see before and after, and can undo.
  • A run tells its story. Moving dashes flow into the step that is working, and turn slow and amber while the run waits for a yes. The line into a step that stopped turns red. Retry picks up at the step that stopped, and a slow step shows how long it has been working. The yes shows where the request went. The new ticket pops out at the end, and a finished run settles into one line. Play “Morning check”, then switch to Bob and approve.
  • The Planner’s test run flows the same way, block to block in n8n. Type “watch Kubernetes every Monday at 9am and create a Jira ticket”.
  • A stopped run answers three things: what happened, what was affected, and whether a retry is safe.
  • Bob sees what will be written and what wasn’t checked, can ask Alice a question, and can decline with a reason. A declined record never says “created”.
  • Needs you is split into Do now and Set up, and stays pinned while you chat.
  • n8n views show only for people with n8n access. Switch “View as” to compare.
  • Try typing “what needs my attention today?”, “is LangGraph up to date?”, “watch Kubernetes and email me”, “make it daily” or “pause the React check”.
